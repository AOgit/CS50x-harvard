time ./sort3 reversed50000.txt
real    0m3.624s
user    0m2.721s
sys     0m0.148s

time ./sort3 random50000.txt
real    0m3.735s
user    0m2.602s
sys     0m0.148s

How do you know?:

Selection sort:
1. Same time for all arrays (sorted, reversed and random) and this time ist longest in compare sort1 und sort2
2. Omega time is biggest for sorted array in compare sort1 und sort2. Omega = n*n for all arrays
